# Access & Accommodations

Stanford University is committed to providing an inclusive and accessible educational environment for all students. Students seeking disability-related accommodations should contact the Office of Accessible Education (OAE) as early as possible to start the process. Once accommodations are approved, provide your accommodation letter to the instructor and discuss how they will be implemented in the course. More at [oae.stanford.edu](http://oae.stanford.edu).

See also the [extension policy](assessments.md#extensions).
