## Class 2 Learning goals

1. Understand the relationship among key ingredients of biosecurity:
   - a. Prevention
   - b. Detection
   - c. Response
   - d. Attribution and Deterrence
2. Understand similarities and differences between biological, chemical, and nuclear weapons (including the concept of “reload”)
3. Be aware of the list of “dual use” “deadly sins” and their recent update by the Trump Administration

## Class 2 advance preparation

1. Look at Brian Tegemoh, Biosecurity Handbook Appendix C: **[Glossary of Biosecurity Terms](https://biosecurityhandbook.com/appendices/glossary.html)**
   - Instructions: Have a look and check out some terms that are unfamiliar to you.
2. Read Richard Danzig **[Catastrophic Bioterrorism: What is to be Done?](https://ndupress.ndu.edu/Portals/68/Documents/occasional/CTNSP/Catastrophic-Bioterrorism.pdf)**
   - Instructions: Read Section 1, pp. 1-2 carefully, peruse the rest optionally. (2 pages)
3. Read Bipartisan Commission on Biodefense, 2024: **[The National Blueprint for Biodefense](https://biodefensecommission.org/wp-content/uploads/2024/05/National-Blueprint-for-Biodefense-2024_final_.pdf)**.
   - Instructions: Read pp. 7-31. (25 pages). I’d prefer you read the full 25 pages but this is an example where an AI summary might suffice.
4. Read webpage: NTI\|bio. **[What is Biosecurity -- Explained](https://www.nti.org/risky-business/what-is-biosecurity-explained/)**
   - Instructions: Read the whole thing (2 pages)
5. Watch **[brief recorded lecture](https://canvas.stanford.edu/courses/231306/files/folder/Videos%20to%20watch%20before%20class?preview=17607727)** on dual use and the seven deadly sins
   - Instructions: Watch the whole thing (5 minutes)
6. Comment on Canvas discussion board. Questions to address include but need not be limited to: What was new to you in these discussions of biosecurity? What do you hope to learn more about during the course of the class? What if anything that you read seemed incomplete or possibly wrong?
