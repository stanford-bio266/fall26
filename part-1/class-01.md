## Class 1 Learning Goals

- Become familiar with the format, assignments, and expectations in the course.
- Understand the course’s working definition of biosecurity, extremely broad and encompassing large-scale natural, accidental and deliberate human communicable disease threats (eg Brown Pandemic Center definition, sometimes called Global Health Security), as well as the existence of narrower definitions focused on deliberate threats
- Become familiar with key facts in the history of biosecurity and their connection to one another

## Class 1 Agenda

1. Intro to the class – biosecurity venn diagram, syllabus
2. Brief history of biosecurity (30 min)
3. Historical figures interactive (if time)
4. Wrap and preview of next class (discussion and guest speaker)
