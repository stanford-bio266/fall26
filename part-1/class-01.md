## Learning goals

- Become familiar with the format, assignments, and expectations in the course.
- Understand the course's working definition of biosecurity: extremely broad, encompassing large-scale natural, accidental, and deliberate human communicable disease threats (e.g. the Brown Pandemic Center definition, sometimes called Global Health Security), as well as narrower definitions focused on deliberate threats.
- Become familiar with key facts in the history of biosecurity and how they connect.

## Before class

No advance preparation. Review the [course info](../course-info/goals-format.md) and the [AI policy](../course-info/ai-policy.md).

## In class

1. Intro to the class: biosecurity Venn diagram, syllabus
2. Brief history of biosecurity (30 min)
3. Historical figures interactive (if time)
4. Wrap-up and preview of next class (discussion and guest speaker)
