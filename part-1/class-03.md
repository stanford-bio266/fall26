## Learning goals

1. Acquire a concrete concept of what could be at stake with biosecurity concerns.
2. Appreciate interdependencies among different components of a response to a catastrophic pandemic and identify vulnerabilities in them.
3. Compare the scenario in Jacobsen's book to your priors and understanding of the risks.

## Before class

### Read

1. **[Biological War: A Scenario](https://www.penguinrandomhouse.com/books/783250/biological-war-by-annie-jacobsen/)**. Annie Jacobsen, Parts 1–2, pp. 1–105. (105 pages)
   - Read all 105 pages. It looks like a lot, but it is very readable, with big print and lots of illustrations. Professor Lipsitch is a slow reader and it took him under 2 hours on a plane. Please actually read it so we can have a good discussion. Where to get it: [Required Books](../course-info/books.md).

### Post on Canvas

Questions you could address include, but are not limited to:

- What new facts or concepts did you learn that surprised you, or that you already knew but found new relevance for?
- What is your assessment of the scenario as a prompt for serious discussion of biosecurity risks? Does it seem realistic? If so, why, given the catastrophe that unfolds? If not, why not?
