## Class 3 Learning Goals

1. To acquire a concrete concept of what could be at stake with biosecurity concerns
2. To appreciate interdependencies among different components of a response to a catastrophic pandemic and identify vulnerabilities in these components.
3. To compare the scenario in Jacobsen’s book to students’ priors and understanding of the risks.

## Class 3 Advance Preparation

1. Read **[Biological War: A Scenario](https://www.penguinrandomhouse.com/books/783250/biological-war-by-annie-jacobsen/)** by Annie Jacobsen, Parts 1-2, pp. 1-105
   - Instructions: Read the whole 105 pages. This looks like a lot of reading. It is super well-written and readable, with big print and lots of illustrations. It’s fast moving and would be good for a beach if you didn’t find it scary. I am a slow reader and it took me <2 hours on a plane to read this part. Please actually read it so we can have a good discussion. I suspect you’ll want to read more, which is why I assigned enough to get you hooked 🙂.
2. Comment about the reading on the Canvas discussion board. Questions you could address include but are not limited to the following:
   - a. What new facts or concepts did you learn that surprised you, or did you already know but found new relevance for?
   - b. What is your assessment of the scenario as a prompt for serious discussion of biosecurity risks? Does it seem realistic? If so why, given the catastrophe that unfolds. If not, why not?
