## Class 10 Learning goals

1. To understand ways in which new technologies amplify biosecurity risks
2. To place these new risks in the context of the history of biological weaponization/terrorism
3. Different kinds of harm: hallucinations; enhancing evildoers; accident + BW;
4. To become aware of extreme bio-attack scenarios considered salient by some experts
