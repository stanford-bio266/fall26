## Before class

### Everyone reads

1. **[Building a Defense-in-Depth Biosecurity Strategy for the AI Era](https://www.rand.org/pubs/research_reports/RRA4999-1.html)**. Guerra S, Attal-Juncqua A, Tarangelo JP, Aveggio C, Dammer K, Glickstein D. RAND Corporation, August 2026. (3 pages and 5 figures)
   - Read the summary (pp. v–vii) and try to decipher the figures. You can, but it takes some concentration.
2. **[Biosecurity Really, Chapter 4](https://www.hoover.org/research/biosecurity-really-strategy-victory)**. Endy et al., 2026. (15 pages)

### Optional

1. **[MicroRNA-based strategy to mitigate the risk of gain-of-function influenza studies](https://www.nature.com/articles/nbt.2666)**. Langlois RA, Albrecht RA, et al., tenOever BR. *Nat Biotechnol* 2013;31(9):844–847.
   - Read it or just the abstract. Different from everything else: focused on safety rather than security, and a very good example of using biotech to reduce the risks of risky research. Maybe not enough, but the impulse is a good and creative one.
2. **['Agentic' life sciences AI is exacerbating bioweapons concerns. Here's what to do about it](https://thebulletin.org/2026/02/agentic-life-sciences-ai-is-exacerbating-bioweapons-concerns-heres-what-to-do-about-it/)**. S. Carter. *Bulletin of the Atomic Scientists*, Feb 26, 2026.
