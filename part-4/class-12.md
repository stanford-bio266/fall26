## Learning goals

1. Know and consider the strengths and weaknesses of the key ideas underlying unfettered, investigator-driven science as a key to innovation.
2. Identify some of the most important practical advances that have come from basic science roots.
3. Discuss the applicability of these principles to solving biosecurity challenges.
4. Consider how biosecurity restrictions may collide with these principles and how the conflict may be managed.
5. Contrast the "blue sky" view with the notion of Pasteur's Quadrant.

## Before class

### 1. Classic texts about "blue sky" science

1. **[Science: The Endless Frontier](https://nsf-gov-resources.nsf.gov/2023-04/EndlessFrontier75th_w.pdf)**. Vannevar Bush, 1945. (17 pages)
   - Read the Summary, Chapters 1–2 (pp. 1–14 in the original report), and the beginning of Chapter 6 (pp. 33–35 in the original).
2. **[The Republic of Science: Its Political and Economic Theory](http://www.jstor.org/stable/41821550)**. M. Polanyi. *Minerva* 1962;1(1):54–73. (5 pages)
   - Read pp. 54–58.

### 2. How basic science feeds applications

1. **[Fundamental science behind today's important medicines](https://www.science.org/doi/10.1126/scitranslmed.aaq1787)**. Spector JM, Harrison RS, Fishman MC. *Sci Transl Med* 2018;10(438):eaaq1787.
2. **Choose one of the following two:**
   - Read **[How restriction enzymes became the workhorses of molecular biology](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC1087929/)**. Roberts RJ. *PNAS* 2005;102(17):5905–8. (4 pages) Written by a Nobel Prize winner, but less scientific content and a little dull.
   - **Or** watch **[The Scissor Servant: Restriction Enzymes and the Birth of Genetic Engineering](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=4JDSg9I_aCs)**. *The Genetic Basis of Stuff and Things*, 2022. Chatty and cartoonish, but scientifically accurate and more science content.

### 3. An update to the classic approach: Pasteur's Quadrant

1. **[Pasteur's Quadrant](https://blog.rootsofprogress.org/pasteurs-quadrant)**. J. Crawford. *Roots of Progress* blog, 2020. (3 pages)
2. *Optional, for a deeper dive and a riff:* **[At the nexus of science, engineering, and medicine: Pasteur's quadrant reconsidered](https://academic.oup.com/pnasnexus/article-lookup/doi/10.1093/pnasnexus/pgac092)**. Pettigrew RI, Cooke JP. *PNAS Nexus* 2022;1(3).
