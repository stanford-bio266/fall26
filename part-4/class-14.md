## Learning goals

1. Apply the concepts of market failure, externalities, and public goods to antimicrobial (antibiotic) resistance and pandemic vaccine availability.
2. Identify the contributing factors to these market failures, and specifically understand why biological systems are often the locus of market failures.
3. Assess and categorize various types of solutions to these challenges.

## Before class

### Watch

1. **[An Introduction to Externalities](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=CpVf11f09Pk)**. A. Tabarrok, Marginal Revolution University. (12:14 at full speed)
   - If you have taken microeconomics, you may be able to skip this.
2. **[A Deeper Look at Public Goods](https://learn.mru.org/lessons/public-goods-definition-nonexcludable-nonrival)**. A. Tabarrok, Marginal Revolution University.
   - If you have taken microeconomics, you may be able to skip this.

> **Note:** These videos are a libertarian take on the topics, particularly in the solutions they propose, and not exactly what Professor Lipsitch would emphasize. But they teach the concepts in a standard way and provide a vocabulary for how we talk about these topics in class as they relate to biosecurity.

### Read

1. **[Establishing a Global Vaccine-Development Fund](https://www.nejm.org/doi/full/10.1056/NEJMp1506820)**. Plotkin SA, Mahmoud AA, Farrar J. *N Engl J Med* 2015;373(4):297–300. (3 pages)
2. **[CEPI 3.0 strategy](https://static.cepi.net/downloads/2026-02/CEPI%203.0%20Strategy%20Report%20DIG%20ENG%20FINAL.pdf)**. Coalition for Epidemic Preparedness Innovations. (2 pages)

3. **[Antibiotic development: economic, regulatory and societal challenges](https://www.nature.com/articles/s41579-019-0293-3.pdf)**. Årdal C, Balasegaram M, Laxminarayan R, et al. *Nat Rev Microbiol* 2020;18:267–274. (8 pages)
4. **[CARB-X: Global Threat](https://carb-x.org/about/global-threat/)**. (1 page)
5. **[CARB-X: Our Strategy](https://carb-x.org/about/our-strategy/)**. (1 page)
