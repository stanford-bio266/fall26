## Class 14 Learning objectives

1. To be able to apply the concepts of market failure, externalities, and public goods to antimicrobial (antibiotic) resistance and pandemic vaccine availability
2. To identify the contributing factors to these market failures, and specifically to understand why biological systems are often the locus of market failures
3. To assess and categorize various types of solutions to these challenges.

## Class 14 Advance preparation

1. Watch Tabarrok A. **[An Introduction to Externalities.](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=CpVf11f09Pk)** Marginal Revolution University (12:14 full speed). If you have taken microeconomics you may be able to skip this.
2. Watch Tabarrok A. **[A Deeper Look at Public Goods.](https://learn.mru.org/lessons/public-goods-definition-nonexcludable-nonrival)** Marginal Revolution University. If you have taken microeconomics you may be able to skip this.

> NOTE: These videos are a libertarian take on the topics particularly in the solutions they propose, not exactly what I’d emphasize. But they teach the concepts in a standard way and provide a vocabulary for how we talk about these topics in class as they relate to the needs in biosecurity.
