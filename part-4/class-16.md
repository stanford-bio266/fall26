## Class 16 learning goals

1. To understand the key considerations in favor of and against performing “enhanced potential pandemic pathogen” or “dangerous gain of function” research.
2. To apply these considerations to a concrete case and appreciate the challenges of doing so.
3. To assess how the debate over this category of studies fits into the larger debate over limiting particular approaches in science for safety and security.
