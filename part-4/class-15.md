## Class 15 Learning goals

1. To understand and evaluate critiques of current American science from the perspective of the replication crisis, lack of coordination, and lack of productivity.
2. To compare alternative, coordinated funding models as they apply to biodefense
3. To learn from a practitioner the challenges of implementing such coordinated funding for the AI x Bio space.

## Class 15 advance preparation

1. Read Daniel Sarewitz 2016. **[Saving science](https://www.thenewatlantis.com/publications/saving-science)**. New Atlantis Spring/Summer, pp. 5-40 (36 pages) Download [here](https://research.ebsco.com/c/qmsjx4/viewer/pdf/dnybx2yxdr?modal=download); seems to take forever.
2. Read Gerard A, Esche M, Turner-Evans D, O’Brien C. 2025. **[How to accelerate the American scientific enterprise](https://ifp.org/accelerating-the-american-scientific-enterprise/)**. Institute for Progress (12 pages)
3. Read Renaissance Philanthropy **[Coordinated research programs](https://www.renaissancephilanthropy.org/playbooks/coordinated-research-programs)** (~5 pages)
4. OPTIONAL: Read or read part (see below) and get an AI summary. K. Gross, & C.T. Bergstrom, **[Why ex post peer review encourages high-risk research while ex ante review discourages it](https://www.pnas.org/doi/10.1073/pnas.2111615118)**, Proc. Natl. Acad. Sci. U.S.A. 118 (51) e2111615118, [https://doi.org/10.1073/pnas.2111615118](https://doi.org/10.1073/pnas.2111615118) (2021). (4 pages if you skip the main math). (This paper is really well written, and you should read from the beginning of the paper up through the end of the first part of the “Theory” section labelled “Evaluating Experiments by Anticipated Shift in Belief” and also the Discussion. The rest iis quite mathematical but makes an important conceptual point about how the system doesn’t work as well as Polanyi would imagine. The math is not the point though it is interesting for some, so this is a great time to ask for help from AI, but you should read the real text at the start and end).
5. Post on Canvas on where you come down on the Sarewitz vs. Bush debate.
