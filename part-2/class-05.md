## Learning goals

1. Develop a view about the kinds of evidence relevant to evaluating interventions for pandemic mitigation.
2. Know and apply multiple criteria or heuristics for asking key questions about interventions proposed to forestall or mitigate pandemics.
3. Evaluate specific candidate interventions according to these criteria.

## In class

1. Framing comments, including discussion of curve-flattening (15 min)
2. Small groups produce two slides (15 min): What is the key idea of this heuristic? What is one example countermeasure, and how does it relate to the heuristic? (It can be the one your reading discussed.)
3. Transpose the matrix: present your heuristic to others in the group and consider the example intervention your paper mentioned (30 min)
   - [Group 1 spreadsheet](https://docs.google.com/spreadsheets/d/1YMLqqQmrlFF2aMNLyw803RaPEx11IuzzHMNCVW1jiLY/edit?usp=sharing)
   - [Group 2 spreadsheet](https://docs.google.com/spreadsheets/d/1hmg2zAMrNDU8KOtTjbCbbo5O9QJasrE1d2hbYWyt6X0/edit?usp=sharing)
4. Whole group: discuss these interventions against the heuristics (35 min): school closure, masking, indoor air purification, vaccination
5. Wrap-up (15 min): timing within the epidemic and changing knowledge; completeness and layering; complementarity (delay spread *and* reinforce defenses; close schools *and* install air purifiers)
