## Class 5 Learning goals

1. To develop a view about kinds of evidence that are relevant to evaluating interventions for pandemic mitigation
2. To know and be able to apply multiple criteria or heuristics to ask key questions about the various kinds of interventions hat may be proposed to forestall or mitigate pandemics.
3. To be able to evaluate specific candidate interventions according to these criteria.
