## Learning goals

1. Identify specific goals a non-pharmaceutical intervention may serve: interrupting transmission in general, interrupting transmission to vulnerable groups, increasing access to care, improving prognosis among those infected, etc.
2. Map these goals, and tradeoffs among them, to specific categories of non-pharmaceutical interventions (partly also in Class 5).
3. Understand the kinds of evidence that may be gathered in advance or in the midst of a pandemic for or against intervention effectiveness.
4. Identify unwanted side effects of non-pharmaceutical interventions and generate ideas to mitigate them, including complementary interventions, timing, and other strategies.
