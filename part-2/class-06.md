## Class 6 learning goals

1. Identify specific goals that a nonpharmaceutical intervention may serve – interrupting transmission (in general), interrupting transmission to vulnerable groups, increasing access to care, improving prognosis among those infected, etc.
2. Be able to map these goals and tradeoffs among them to specific categories of nonpharmaceutical interventions (partly also in Class 5)
3. Understand the various kinds of evidence that may be gathered in advance or in the midst of a pandemic for or against intervention effectiveness.
4. Identify unwanted side effects of nonpharmaceutical interventions and generate ideas for approaches to mitigating them, including complementary interventions, timing, and other strategies.
5. *To be added.*

Epub 2020 Jul 29. PMID: 32726550.
   - Instructions: Read the whole thing. (5 pages)
