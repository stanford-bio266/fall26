## Class 4 Learning goals

1. To be able to recognize, critically evaluate, and use concepts from epidemiological modeling in the context of pandemics.
2. To understand how the prevention and mitigation of pandemics depends on phase, with different goals at different phases
3. To appreciate how communication style can influence a population’s response to pandemic interventions
4. To discuss and form a view of the evidence base on which pandemic interventions can be based and the presence of irreducible uncertainty.
5. To be able to define potential costs and benefits of pandemic interventions and discuss how they can be weighed
6. To apply the concept of offramps and regular decision points to pandemic decision making.

## Class 4 Agenda

1. Intro to this section of the course, and forming groups for reading for next class
2. Questions/discussion about the video mini-lecture
3. Rotating small group discussions drawing on the readings and experience:
   - a. Small group discussion on lessons observed from COVID-19 in each of 6 areas:
     - i. Group 1: in the areas of 1) communication and 2) collateral damage
     - ii. Group 2: in the areas of 3) decision making under uncertainty and 4) individual leadership
     - iii. Group 3: in the areas of 5) scientific preparedness and 6) government powers/structures
   - b. Rotate groups and discuss whether the US/ the world is more or less prepared on each of these dimensions
   - c. Rotate groups and come up with one action in “peacetime” that would help us do better the next time.
4. Summary of learning goals and findings
