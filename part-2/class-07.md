## Learning goals for Class 7

1. To understand the value and limitations of vaccines in pandemic response
2. To understand the key steps of vaccine development and approval, why this process has historically taken a long time, and why it was possible for COVID-19 vaccines to roll out (in some countries) in less than a year; as well as current efforts to speed this process further and challenges to equitable global distribution..
3. To understand the state of preparedness for vaccines against a set of high-risk potential pandemic pathogens.
4. To anticipate what will and will not be known at various stages of vaccine deployment in emergencies about the properties of the vaccines, and how this affects their usage
5. To be able to make evidence-based prioritization of vaccines within and between populations amidst this uncertainty.
